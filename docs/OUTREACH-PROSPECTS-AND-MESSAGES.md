## What this list is

This is a curated first outreach batch for finding technically useful design partners, independent methodological critics, and regulated-pathway advisers. It is not a claim that any organization endorses Outsider, has reviewed it, will share data, or will provide underwriting, guarantees, claims settlement, licensed issuance, or capital custody.

Outsider is described here with the narrowest accurate boundary: a **source-replayed real-evidence system**. It preserves real cases and real experience as real evidence, while separately tracking source quality, measurement reliability, causal strength, target-domain transfer, data rights, and operational authority. Public origin does not downgrade a real case; Outsider's own collection does not upgrade weak evidence. The deciding criteria are what the data actually are, how they were produced, and whether provenance, quality, authority, and use rights survive review.

## Selection and data-quality rules

- Only an official first-party page, an official organization-level form, a public role inbox, or an official organization LinkedIn page is admitted.
- No private phone number, personal mailbox, scraped employee record, guessed email address, or data-broker enrichment is present.
- Every channel was browsed on the verification date. “Public active” means the route was visibly published; it does not mean the organization will answer.
- Every organization has one specific, falsifiable starting pilot. A generic “partnership” is not treated as evidence of fit.
- First agent and robot pilots are bounded, consented, pre-registered, and read-only. They do not command production systems and do not automatically contribute data to training.
- Robotics and claims contacts carry elevated safety, confidentiality, worker/claimant-standing, privacy, or regulated-authority risks.
- Data access always requires a separate rights, consent, security, retention, and privacy agreement.
- Dynamic forms and program timing can change. Re-open the official source immediately before outreach; Lloyd's Lab is specifically marked timing-dependent, and official LinkedIn may require login.
- This is a high-quality first batch, not a defensible claim to have enumerated every organization on the web.

## Design-partner data and evidence request

This is the one-page scoping sheet to send only after a prospect asks what a pilot needs. It defines three executable tracks. Select exactly one; do not combine their permissions or silently expand scope.

### A. Agent platform: role-scoped bounded evidence pilot (maximum 30 days)

**Mode:** choose exactly one organization-specific submode: consented trace shadow/replay, a frozen non-production agent suite, a non-production sandbox experiment, or annotation/adjudication protocol design. The window is at most 30 calendar days after protocol freeze. Outsider does not intercept production; sandbox fault injection never runs against production.

**Minimum requested evidence**

- Opaque episode ID and the eligibility/exposure denominator: which runs were in scope and why.
- Minimized timestamps and categories for action/tool type, potential side effect, and environment/version bucket.
- Pre-agreed outcome definition; outcome status, evidence source, and observation time.
- Human intervention/correction category, retry state, and whether recovery held.
- Missing/censored status and prospective-holdout membership.
- A data-lineage and rights manifest covering consent, retention, deletion, and approved use.
- When side effects can affect a customer, employee, or end user: affected-party standing, notice, an objection/withdrawal path, and consent or another documented lawful basis.

**Explicitly not requested:** raw prompts, raw completions, source code, model weights, secrets, credentials, unrestricted production access, customer/employee/end-user PII, or unrelated payload contents. No evidence enters training without a separate affirmative agreement.

**Success:** the denominator and protocol are frozen before observation; valid minimized records produce deterministic episode roots and replayable receipts; the prospective holdout reports outcome, correction, recovery, missingness, and all eligible failures; production behavior is unchanged. A performance gain is not required.

**Stop:** rights/consent cannot be demonstrated; applicable affected-party standing, notice, lawful basis, or objection/withdrawal handling is absent or withdrawn; sensitive payload or identity data exceeds the minimum; collection affects production, security, or user safety; or eligibility/outcome definitions change without re-registration.

### B. Robot lab/company: retrospective plus prospective replay

**Mode:** phase one replays an agreed retrospective export; phase two observes a pre-registered prospective holdout. Both phases are read-only. Existing operators and safety systems retain authority; Outsider receives no actuator path.

**Minimum requested evidence**

- Agreed source-evidence bytes accessible to Outsider or an independent replay runner in a controlled environment. If bytes stay on site, require an immutable byte manifest plus a deterministic source-aware replay attestation; a digest alone is insufficient and is never called source replay.
- Episode root, pseudonymous asset/session ID, and task/exposure denominator.
- Task start/end; environment and hardware/software version categories; sensor/evidence missingness.
- Pre-agreed outcome plus the result recorded by a designated site or independent observer.
- Operator intervention/correction, near miss, repeated failure, downtime, and recovery state.
- Affected-party standing and consent receipt without embedding names or personal details.
- Prospective eligibility frozen before prospective outcomes are visible.

**Explicitly not requested:** robot source code, proprietary weights, credentials, actuator commands, unbounded fleet telemetry, or production control. Raw video/audio, faces, voices, biometrics, worker/customer identifiers, and evidence without site rights or required consent are excluded by default. If a narrowly necessary media field is later proposed, it needs a separate minimization and consent review.

**Success:** retrospective bytes are hash-bound, episode-rooted, and replayable; the prospective denominator/outcome/stop rules are pre-registered; no actuation occurs; affected-party consent and outcome receipts are attached; all failures, missing episodes, and retrospective/prospective disagreement remain visible.

**Stop:** any actuator or safety-control path becomes possible; site/worker/bystander/data rights are missing or withdrawn; the export exposes unapproved personal, biometric, security-sensitive, or trade-secret data; or labels/eligibility change after results are visible.

### C. Methods, insurer, claims, or capital partner: reference review

**Mode:** one or two scoped sessions as an explicit methods critic, actuarial-model reviewer, claims-lifecycle reviewer, or regulated-pathway reviewer. It is not underwriting, binding, actuarial sign-off, claim adjudication, payment, licensed issuance, carrier capacity, or capital custody.

**Minimum requested evidence**

- Outsider provides its model specification, factor dictionary, assumptions, units, limitations, and de-identified example receipts.
- Review covers exposure/loss/outcome/recovery definitions; calibration, validation, tail, dependence, transfer, missingness, and uncertainty diagnostics.
- Outsider provides data-rights, control, role, authority, audit, correction, and version matrices.
- For claims specialists, use a reference lifecycle from notice through evidence, assignment, decision, remedy/payment status, recovery, reopen, and closure.
- The reviewer is asked to mark model defects, missing evidence, required licensed roles, claims controls, capacity/custody steps, and unresolved disagreements.

**Explicitly not requested:** claimant/customer PII, live claim files, privileged material without contract, production credentials, capital transfer or account access, premium collection, quote/bind/underwrite/guarantee/adjudicate/pay/settle authority, or permission to represent review as approval or capacity.

**Success:** written redlines trace claims to evidence and assumptions; missing data is separated from missing authority; next evidence and institutional roles are named; disagreement/rejection is retained; the review ends with zero quote, binding, settlement, custody, or authority claim by Outsider.

**Stop:** the review would be represented as regulatory, actuarial, carrier, or capital approval; it requires uncontracted claimant/privileged data; Outsider is asked to perform a licensed, fiduciary, claims, payment, or custody function; or reference data lack documented rights.

## Personalization protocol

Before using any template:

1. Replace `[Organization]`, `[Team]`, and `[specific pilot]` with the exact catalog entry; do not send unresolved placeholders.
2. Lead with one reason that is visible on the organization's official page. Do not manufacture familiarity, referrals, customer status, or privileged access.
3. Attach at most a two-page protocol: scope, read-only boundary, data fields, outcome definition, holdout, failure-retention rule, security, deletion, and stop conditions.
4. Ask for one small decision: the correct team, a 20–30 minute fit call, or permission to send the protocol.
5. Do not attach non-public data. Do not request production credentials, live actuator access, claimant records, or employee data in the first exchange.
6. Record response, consent, and rejection separately. Silence is not consent. A reply is not a partnership or validation.
