# Real responsibility math diagnostics

This layer refines Outsider's factor and loss mathematics with source-replayed real cases and real institutional experience. It does not relabel public real-world evidence as less real merely because Outsider found it online. It also does not elevate evidence merely because Outsider collected or transformed it. Descriptive claim admission is claim-specific and depends on byte identity, provenance, measurement definition, population frame, revision handling, missingness, denominator fit, temporality, and adjudication. Rights and consent do not change whether the recorded event was real, but they can restrict reuse, training, and operational admission; target transfer remains a separate requirement.

The artifact is `outsider/real-responsibility-math-diagnostics/v1`. It is deliberately a set of separate source-domain diagnostics, not a global risk score and not a target-domain pricing model.

## Source-aware construction

Full construction does the following in one invocation:

1. Stable-reads the caller-named frozen catalog and responsibility-flywheel canonicals with no symlink following and pins their semantic hashes, serialized hashes, serialized byte counts, and timestamps independently of the math timestamp.
2. Structurally reproduces both frozen canonicals from code and exact-checks the flywheel-to-catalog binding.
3. Calls the flywheel full verifier on the frozen canonical with source paths only. The verifier must replay all sixteen source profiles and twenty-six lanes in the same call and exact-compare its rebuilt flywheel to the frozen canonical.
4. Requires its generated `replayedReceipts` for every pinned upstream artifact, including contained lanes, exact resolved artifact path and source profile, path roles, file bytes, file hash, semantic artifact hash, source commitment, verifier level, and successful exact rebuild. The replayed lane set and catalog binding must also match the frozen flywheel.
5. Discards caller upstream-artifact paths after replay and re-reads only the paths returned by those internally generated receipts, using stable regular-file reads and checking file bytes plus semantic artifact hashes.
6. Re-extracts a small fixed fact projection and rejects any drift.
7. Recomputes all ratios, identities, empirical summaries, censoring flags, missingness fractions, and loss fragments.
8. Exact-compares the rebuilt diagnostics artifact.

The exact frozen catalog is `artifacts/real-case-gap-evidence-catalog-20260828/final-v18/catalog.json`, catalog hash `sha256:b690ddeb4a9a01b94938f6558dfe565e4f6703d16eb22e0b05fb88d9c32d6f66`, serialized hash `sha256:cce91100a329ad2d8687945d48e3c8774aa6f9a4ed6ccdc077fe5b9b9fc20eb0`, and 69,059 bytes. The exact frozen flywheel is `artifacts/real-case-responsibility-flywheel-20260828/final-v16/flywheel.json`, semantic hash `sha256:0a80bf8bd7ad41fa49423a01ec2af3667510b2fa7dcb0f72a234adf1451fcd77`, serialized hash `sha256:18655bfce66c23d12c300fc7a9bc88a618bb240b0fcf07ca85a634a1aa358f68`, 644,539 bytes, schema version `1.7.0`, and timestamp `2026-08-28T08:40:00.720Z`. The math artifact has the separate code-fixed timestamp `2026-08-28T09:03:30.091Z`. Projection, source build, standalone verification, and full verification reject a caller-selected alternative timestamp, including coherent past or future reseals.

No API accepts a caller trust boolean, verifier callback, prior verification result, pre-built flywheel, or external receipt. Standalone verification reconstructs the fixed dictionary, factor-admission gates, math, and boundaries, but reports `sourceArtifactsReverified: false`.

Browsable-only catalog references are not numerical model inputs. A domain enters this layer only after it has a frozen source-aware adapter, exact artifact/source commitments, and a full replay path. Wave5, BridgeData V2, Waymo, and ClinicalTrials.gov now join the existing source-replayed adapters. Wave5 contributes three retrospective taxonomy/missingness factors over 49 source-replayed rows; all are unavailable pre-action and admit zero trainer rows, target predictors, prices, or authority. BridgeData contributes two non-model candidate-quality factors and keeps `trainerRowsEnqueued = 0`; it does not establish full-corpus semantics, outcome labels, correlation groups, target transfer, or capability improvement. Waymo contributes seven bounded source-domain outcome/exposure, missingness, revision, and benchmark-reference factors; displayed miles remain rounded, publisher SGO membership rows remain non-independent, duplicate/conflicting rows and 164 literal nonfinite tokens remain quarantined, and the retrospective calendar split is not a prospective or external target holdout. ClinicalTrials.gov contributes three study-rooted posted-results factors, with no cross-study pooling or causal claim; sponsor/person names are stripped, raw named records are not semantically committed, and unreplayed rights pages keep rights, training, target transfer, and price unestablished. None of these sources receives target, price, capital, settlement, or authority admission.

## Typed retrospective factors

The dictionary contains nine factor families:

- `OUTCOME_SENSING`: reported MAUDE event-type composition, the bounded DMV collision-form sample, NTSB case-specific final safety findings, separate BLS SOII/CFOI outcomes, the GAO recommendation-nonimplementation counterexample, MSHA reported accident rows, FRA reported outcome/rate/damage-cost diagnostics, separate NHTSA quarterly and annual manufacturer-status compositions, and Waymo SGO membership plus publisher benchmark-factor profiles.
- `REVISION`: MAUDE add/change precedence, follow-up evidence, FDIC downward cumulative revisions, the absent CPSC longitudinal revision chain, the DMV enhanced-reporting regime transition, NTSB post-freeze revision status, OIICS version/series-break context, the Judgment Fund current-snapshot/no-version-history boundary, NCUA preliminary-versus-audited scope separation, FRA canonical-query plus deduplication/correlation boundaries, NHTSA campaign dedup plus latest-status selection, and Waymo duplicate-pair plus retrospective-split boundaries.
- `MISSINGNESS`: the existing source-domain gaps plus three Wave5 session-shape, ROS mechanism and rights/pre-action admission profiles, Waymo nonfinite tokens and dynamic-benchmark duplicate/conflict keys, and two BridgeData non-model candidate-quality profiles for one-record coverage and unresolved semantics/correlation/rights/training gates.
- `EXPOSURE`: RMA cell-recorded exposure counts, legacy DMV public-road test miles, the temporally unmatched current NFIP contract snapshot, separate BLS SOII/CFOI exposure frames, MSHA quarterly hours, CDI filed earned exposures, FRA calendar-year train miles and employee hours, and Waymo's 220,613,000-mile displayed rounded point.
- `PAYMENT`: NFIP paid amounts and latency, RMA aggregate indemnity, CAS cumulative-paid backtest error, CFPB distributions, FTC sent payments, Treasury Judgment Fund payment-component amounts, and separately framed PBGC direct, traditional, and SFA payments.
- `RECOVERY`: NFIP recorded recoveries, FDIC dividends and payment latency, FTC cashed payments, CPSC firm-reported corrections, and PBGC SFA death-audit repayments kept outside ordinary claim-recovery semantics.
- `ADJUDICATION`: FEMA second-appeal status and signed-decision duration, NTSB adopted-report safety-investigation finality kept separate from legal liability, GAO sustain/100-day aggregates kept separate from judicial finality and implementation, and CDI acceptance kept separate from approval and charged policy price.
- `CAPITAL`: FDIC resolution-cost diagnostics, selected-insurer reserve/capital disclosures, NCUA audited fund position and insurance-liability boundaries, and NCIGF assessment capacity and reconciliation boundaries.
- `CUSTODY`: Treasury collateral roles, Judgment Fund certification/setoff/disbursement roles, a selected insurer's restricted regulatory deposit, and CFPB's fiduciary-fund description.

Every factor has a value type, unit, measurement scope, temporality, source domain, source lane, quality prerequisites, missingness policy, and interpretation boundary. Every factor is retrospective. Outcome-derived factors are machine-marked as unavailable before action. Every factor has:

```text
targetDomainTransferEstablished = false
targetPredictorEligible = false
targetPriceEligible = false
crossDomainPoolingPermitted = false
```

The projection evaluates every prerequisite against the fixed flywheel quality assessment for the same lane. This is a set of minimum-status gates, not a score: there is no numeric weight, collector-prestige adjustment, public-source penalty, or Outsider-collection bonus. A factor can be admitted only for its stated bounded retrospective source-domain use; target-domain use stays false. Full verification binds those admissions to the same-call source-replayed flywheel.

Every diagnostic section also carries its own local boundary: bounded retrospective description is admitted for source-domain diagnostics, while cross-domain/cross-publisher pooling, target transfer, predictor eligibility, price eligibility, and operational authority are false. The PHMSA receipt-only section is stricter: it admits only receipt identity and the explicit deferral boundary, not bounded outcome description or model input. NHTSA admits frozen source-domain record and status composition only; manufacturer-reported cumulative counts remain distinct from affected-party receipt, independently verified complete repair, current vehicle exposure, paid/incurred/ultimate loss, and enforcement. These restrictions therefore do not rely only on a global disclaimer.

## Admitted source-domain mathematics

The current fixed diagnostics include:

## Verification and attacks

The test suite covers:

- heterogeneous dollar/count/rate pooling after caller resealing;
- same-lane CFPB/FTC/CPSC pooling and arbitrary numeric quality weights;
- a post-outcome factor relabeled as a pre-action predictor;
- a temporally mismatched NFIP denominator relabeled as frequency;
- selected DMV forms divided by legacy miles;
- Judgment Fund components relabeled as claims or exposure, and GAO filed/closed cohorts or rounded rates given invented operands;
- NCUA preliminary figures promoted to audited capital or reserve/provision figures promoted to paid losses;
- PBGC heterogeneous populations pooled into a common severity, or SFA repayments relabeled ordinary claim recovery;
- MSHA Q3 outcomes divided by Q2 exposure;
- CDI proposed/accepted values relabeled approved or charged, including silent repair of the one-dollar filed-total discrepancy;
- NCIGF assessment capacity relabeled cash, Outsider capital, custody, independently verified value, or a target-domain price;
- FRA reported damage relabeled paid/incurred/ultimate loss, or its bounded calendar-year rate promoted to a target rate or price;
- PHMSA captured rows relabeled unique incidents, divided by FRA exposure, or promoted to a factor, frequency, severity, loss fragment, training input, or price;
- NHTSA cumulative manufacturer-status composition relabeled complete repair or affected-party receipt, quarterly and annual cohorts pooled, recall/status populations used as target exposure, or counts promoted to frequency, severity, loss, enforcement, training, or price;
- Waymo displayed rounded miles relabeled exact exposure, SGO membership rows relabeled independent events, publisher factors relabeled Outsider calibration, conflicting windows pooled, or the retrospective split promoted to a prospective/external target holdout;
- BridgeData's one candidate record promoted to full-corpus semantics, independent truth, trainer admission, model improvement, target transfer, or price;
- unknown top-level, nested diagnostic, and dictionary-factor fields after coherent semantic resealing;
- missing, stale, or byte-reserialized catalog canonicals;
- byte drift in the Wave5, BridgeData, Waymo, or ClinicalTrials.gov upstream canonical artifacts;
- drift in the FRA/PHMSA acquisition-manifest binding;
- drift in the NHTSA acquisition-manifest binding;
- a stale embedded flywheel receipt;
- a wrong frozen-canonical semantic hash after caller resealing;
- a semantic-equivalent but byte-different flywheel reserialization;
- a stale superseded flywheel path;
- coherent 2000 and 2099 generated-time reseals against projection, source builder, standalone verification, and full verification;
- authority promotion after caller resealing;
- caller-provided booleans, callbacks, and verification results;
- byte drift in a nominally identical upstream JSON file before expensive source replay;
- full same-call flywheel source replay and diagnostic recomputation.
