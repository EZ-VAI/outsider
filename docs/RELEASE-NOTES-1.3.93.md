# Outsider 1.3.93

1.3.93 is one bounded release-gate repair derived from the complete, sealed
1.3.92 formal R5 run. The run was allowed to reach its own terminal after
2 hours 20 minutes. No product source, evaluator, threshold, fixture or release
artifact changed while it was live, and it was not restarted after a partial
result.

The immutable 1.3.92 artifact proved the difficult product properties:

- a real two-member Sonnet Agent Team created exactly three frozen tasks;
- each teammate owned, changed, tested and independently completed its bound
  slice before lead integration;
- the controller survived a real SIGKILL and resumed the same run and seal;
- an evaluator-owned recovery fault produced one factually audited correction,
  delivery observation, matching effect, independent outcome approval and
  same-authority intervention resolution;
- all 13 externally timed checkpoints were at least ten minutes apart and the
  witnessed duration exceeded two hours;
- the final mechanical acceptance, semantic outcome, causal proof and Agent Team
  conformance passed;
- the evidence manifest verified immediately and after finalization, with no
  post-terminal event append or source-hash drift.

The run was not certified as R5. It produced two usable semantic patrol verdicts
where the preregistration required four. The remaining patrol boundaries became
due, but the controller correctly withheld their model calls to preserve the
completion-only reserve. This was a conservative evaluation failure, not a
false delivery or a failed intervention.

The root cause was arithmetic in the formal endurance budget. It modeled every
patrol as a two-call happy path and did not separately budget the bounded work
that follows when a patrol finds a real defect. The live run exercised that
branch: the first patrol emitted a valid correction, and its later follow-up,
clearance re-diagnosis and independent outcome closure consumed calls that the
preregistration had implicitly promised to patrols three and four.

1.3.93 keeps the four-patrol requirement unchanged and replaces the optimistic
total with an explicit worst-branch ledger. It separately accounts for:

- two teammate delivery clearances, including rejected-clearance re-diagnosis;
- four authoritative patrol verdicts, including a rejected first draft;
- one patrol correction's bounded follow-up and outcome closure;
- the recovery correction and its outcome checks;
- integration/recovery-continuation outcomes; and
- an untouched eight-call final completion reserve.

The complete derivation is frozen into every new R5 preregistration rather than
publishing only the total. A regression based on the sealed 1.3.92 call profile
proves that one real patrol correction cannot make the remaining required patrols
structurally unreachable.

Verification on release source is 453/453 unit, integration, authenticated IPC,
Agent Team and SIGKILL recovery tests plus 125/125 deterministic gate-corpus
cases. The 1.3.92 run remains permanently verifiable and is retained as a valid
safe-delivery, closed-loop and endurance observation, but not as a passing R5
certification sample.

R5 evidence is artifact- and version-scoped. A fresh formal R5 on the immutable
1.3.93 artifact is required before stable public-release readiness can be
claimed. This release note deliberately does not promote the prior run or lower
the gate.
