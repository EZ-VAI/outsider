# Real-case gap evidence catalog

The readiness ledger contains 36 target-specific gaps whose truth cannot be
created merely by implementing another local parser or state machine: 19
evidence gaps, 15 institutional-authority gaps, and two platform-capability
gaps. Their observable real-world components can and should be filled by
quality-gated public evidence; only the exact target adoption, execution or
authorization fact remains unsatisfied when that fact has not happened.

`src/outsider-real-case-gap-evidence-catalog.js` prevents two opposite errors:

- treating real public cases as “only research” because Outsider did not
  collect them; and
- treating public examples of an institution as if that institution had
  adopted Outsider.

For each gap the catalog records what primary, browsable sources can establish
about the real phenomenon and the remaining target-specific fact. Examples:

- OSHA, NHTSA, FDA MAUDE and California DMV establish real physical outcome
  cases and reporting regimes; they do not create an outcome sensor bound to an
  Outsider execution.
- RMA, NFIP and Schedule P establish real exposure, paid/incurred loss and
  reserve-development structures in their source domains; they do not by
  themselves estimate an AI/robot target-domain frequency.
- FEMA appeals, CBCA arbitrations, CFPB responses, DOJ judgments and EEOC
  settlements establish real challenge, adjudication and remedy lifecycles.
  The CBCA FEMA panel is external to FEMA and its rules describe an impartial
  process; the frozen evidence does not establish Executive Branch
  institutional independence. None of these sources appoints an Outsider
  claims institution.
- GAO bid-protest decisions and annual disposition statistics add a separate
  real challenge/corrective-action precedent. They remain procurement-domain
  records, not an appointment to adjudicate Outsider obligations.
- CFPB harmed-consumer payments, FTC refund programs and CPSC monthly recall
  progress establish real allocation, payment-administration and remedy
  lifecycles. They do not prove that every eligible party was reached or that
  an Outsider obligation was restored and accepted.
- NHTSA's source-replayed post-2010 recall, quarterly status and annual status
  files add a real campaign-to-remedy-status lifecycle. Manufacturer-reported
  status is retained with population mismatches and malformed periods; it is
  not treated as an affected-party receipt, complete repair, independent causal
  finding, loss record, enforcement action or consent order.
- The Wave5 source-aware intake adds 49 source-replayed real-software taxonomy
  rows: three Trace Commons session shapes and 46 ROBUST ROS bug/fix mechanism
  records. They sharpen software failure, missingness, rights and reuse-gap
  descriptions only. Every row is retrospective and lacks an independent
  outcome, exposure denominator and economic loss; zero rows are pre-action,
  trainer, target-predictor or PRICE eligible, and no authority is granted.
- BridgeData V2 adds a real Berkeley lab-robot trajectory and publisher TFDS
  metadata. The frozen adapter decodes exactly one complete TFRecord while the
  published train split contains 53,192 examples across 1,024 shards. The
  source is a quality-gated training candidate on `HOLD`, not a training
  admission: the complete corpus, semantic contract, correlation structure and
  target transfer remain unestablished, and zero rows enter any trainer.
- Waymo Safety Impact adds a separate, non-pooled operational exposure and
  outcome reference: 220.613 million displayed rounded Rider-Only miles,
  SGO-linked publisher outcome rows and published benchmark comparisons. The
  adapter retains 164 literal `NaN` values and duplicate identifier, outcome,
  location and benchmark conflicts. Exact publisher replay does not establish
  underlying truth, independent adjudication, target transfer or price.
- ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 adds a complete frozen query window of 18 study
  records whose aggregate results were first posted on 2026-07-01. The exact
  pagination chain, field metadata, study roots, 242 outcome-measure rows, 409
  serious-adverse-event terms, and 514 other-adverse-event terms are replayed.
  These remain sponsor- or investigator-supplied registry postings: NLM quality
  control is not scientific-accuracy adjudication, and no study, term, group,
  affected count, or rate is pooled across studies. The derived lane strips
  sponsor/person names and does not commit raw named records. Terms and
  disclaimer pages are URL-only and were not replayed, so rights are
  `NOT_ESTABLISHED_URL_ONLY_NOT_REPLAYED`; causality, training permission,
  target transfer, price, and authority remain false.
- ASOPs, NAIC repositories, SEC insurer filings, FDIC Call Reports and Treasury
  data establish real pricing, capital, reserve, restricted-deposit, custody
  and payment structures; they do not fund or authorize an Outsider guarantee.
- NCUA Share Insurance Fund statements add audited federal deposit-insurance
  assets, net position, reserves, insured shares and insurance-loss experience.
  PBGC open data adds insured-plan exposure, trusteeship, claims, financial
  assistance and direct/indirect benefit payments. These are real institutional
  operations, while their program definitions and balance sheets remain
  non-transferable to an Outsider obligation without a separate bridge.
- The Treasury Judgment Fund process separately records the institutional
  sequence from a final award through eligibility review, offset, certified
  voucher and disbursing official. It is a real payment-control precedent, not
  an Outsider custodian receipt. Its statutory biweekly reports additionally
  expose real final-award payment records. The source-aware adapter now replays
  14 contiguous 2026 reports through July and publishes only privacy-minimized
  agency/citation aggregates; payment components remain distinct from unique
  claims, exposure, recipient settlement and recovery.
- California DOI/SERFF filings expose real earned-premium, incurred-claim,
  proposed-rate and actuarial-certification structures; NCIGF reports expose
  statutory guaranty-fund capacity, assessable premium, assessments and
  refunds, while also disclosing that inputs and estimates are not independently
  verified by the publisher.
- The source-replayed 2024 BLS IIF lane preserves SOII/CFOI counts, hours/FTE
  denominators, RSEs, suppression, publication thresholds and classification
  versions. The source-replayed NTSB lane preserves six completed partial-
  automation investigations, five final reports, docket roots and publisher
  typo quarantines. The FRA lane now source-replays seven exact canonical SoQL
  receipts for 2015–2019 Form 54 representative accident records and same-year
  Form 55 operational exposure. Reported damage remains distinct from paid,
  incurred or ultimate loss. The PHMSA lane preserves a real byte-replayed
  gas-distribution incident snapshot, but keeps frequency, severity and pricing
  inputs machine-deferred until matching annual exposure, revision and rights
  bindings exist.
  MSHA accident/injury/illness and employment files add another official real
  workplace source family. Reporting regimes and population frames are never
  silently pooled.
- The Codex app-server schema plus the real zero-model local-host probe record
  what the installed host actually exposes: the current hooks remain untrusted,
  exact hook-to-item identity is absent, and control remains `UNCONTROLLED`.
  The DeepSeek Cordis canary records a real cooperative delivery path, while
  explicitly withholding semantic-outcome, causal-effect and authority claims.

The catalog covers every gap, but does not claim it has searched an unbounded,
continuously changing internet exhaustively. It records a repeatable snapshot
of primary-source families and is designed to be rerun and extended.

Its standalone verifier checks the code-fixed catalog only. Source-aware
adapters remain responsible for downloading, pinning, replaying and validating
each dataset before rows enter a factor, loss, capital or adjudication lane.
Every source marked `ADAPTER_SOURCE_REPLAYED_LOCAL` carries both a current
local hash and a machine-readable hash kind; a browsable URL without such a
binding is kept at `OFFICIAL_BROWSABLE_SOURCE_IDENTIFIED`.
